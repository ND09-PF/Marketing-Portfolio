About Me ✨

I've worked across pharma, automotive, entertainment, semiconductor, and digital, both in companies and agencies, so I've had to adapt to very different kinds of businesses and audiences.

Each role has been different, but what's stayed consistent is the need to understand the context, what the business is trying to achieve, who the customer is, and where marketing can actually make an impact.

I'm interested in how marketing decisions are made, and how data, intuition, and experimentation factor into those decisions.

How I Got Here 🛍️

The story behind
the curiosity

Growing up, I used to go shopping with my mom, and I'd often notice how similar products competed with each other. I was curious about what made someone choose one brand over another, and what actually influenced that decision.

As a consumer, I started noticing how marketing shaped my own choices too, from ads to packaging to the way brands positioned themselves. That's where my interest in marketing really began.

Over time, that curiosity turned into a career. I started paying closer attention to how brands communicate and how campaigns influence behavior, and eventually moved into roles where I could work on these problems directly.

How I Work 🧠

The way I approach things

01
Start with context
I begin by understanding what the business needs, who the customer is, and how competitors are positioned. This helps identify where marketing can actually make an impact.
02
Test, learn, and iterate
I rely on data and past performance to guide decisions. I look at what has worked, what hasn't, and use testing to improve results instead of making assumptions.
03
Focus on what drives outcomes
I prioritise what actually moves performance, not just activity. I enjoy working on problems where there isn't a clear answer upfront and figuring things out over time.
