Case Study · Automotive

Scaling Test Drive Conversions

Mercedes-Benz Auto Hangar  ·  2021–2023

Drove digital campaigns to improve lead quality and increase test drive conversions for a Mercedes-Benz dealership.

Industry
Automotive · Luxury
Role
Marketing Manager
Channels
Meta · Google Ads · CRM
Duration
2 Years
Problem

What we were working against

Strong lead volume, but inconsistent conversion into test drives. It wasn't clear which leads were more likely to convert and which ones were not.

The challenge was to identify high-intent audiences and improve campaign efficiency by focusing on users more likely to take action.

Action

What we did about it

  • Built a structured funnel across Meta and Google Ads, moving users from enquiry to showroom visit to test drive
  • Designed audience targeting using demographics, interests, behavioral signals, and high-intent keyword strategy
  • Used CRM data from HubSpot to analyze conversion patterns and identify high-intent customer segments
  • Created acquisition cohorts such as existing Mercedes owners, high-engagement leads, and users showing repeated interest
  • Continuously optimized campaigns using performance data, testing, and audience refinement
Results

What the numbers showed

1.2K–1.5K
Qualified leads generated per month
~65%
Lead qualification rate achieved
100–150
Test drives driven consistently each month
Better
targeting
Improved campaign efficiency through data-driven decisions
Key Takeaway

Focusing on high-intent audience segments and continuously refining targeting based on data can significantly improve conversion outcomes without increasing spend.
